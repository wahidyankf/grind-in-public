# Hiring, Feedback, and Development

People processes should be clear, evidence-based, timely, and humane. Ambiguity feels easier in the moment but creates
unfairness and delayed surprises.

## Hiring system

1. Define the work, level, must-have capabilities, and evidence before opening a role.
2. Use structured interviews with distinct competencies and anchored scoring.
3. Train interviewers and calibrate using evidence, not intuition or pedigree.
4. Make independent scorecards before debrief to reduce conformity.
5. Audit pass-through and candidate experience for unintended barriers.
6. Onboard against 30/60/90-day outcomes and relationships, not an information dump.

Reject duplicate "culture fit" interviews; assess explicit values through behaviour and add perspective rather than
similarity.

## Feedback model

```text
context -> observed behaviour -> impact -> expectation -> listen -> agreement/support -> follow-up
```

Example: "In the last two design reviews, the proposal arrived after implementation had begun. That removed the team's
ability to change the boundary safely. For cross-service changes, I expect the RFC before coding. What got in the way,
and what support would make that workable?"

Describe behaviour, not personality. Give reinforcing feedback as specifically as corrective feedback.

## Development plans

Connect aspiration, current evidence, next-level expectation, stretch work, support, and observable checkpoints. A plan
may combine ownership of a bounded design, mentoring, incident leadership, communication practice, and targeted study.
Promotion follows sustained next-level impact; it is not a reward for tenure or one heroic project.

## Performance concerns

Act early. Clarify expectations and gaps with examples, hear context, provide reasonable support, set bounded observable
checkpoints, document consistently, and follow organizational process. Protect confidentiality while ensuring the team
still receives necessary direction.

## Team health

Use recurring one-to-ones for the person's agenda, feedback, growth, obstacles, and context—not task status. Watch
on-call burden, meeting load, work distribution, belonging, attrition signals, and single points of knowledge.
