# Scenario 007: Hiring a Distributed-Systems Team

You must form a team to operate high-throughput Python services on Kubernetes. Current engineers know the domain but
have limited distributed-systems experience. Leadership initially asks for five identical senior hires.

Explain the capability map, build-versus-hire choices, role mix, structured interview loop, calibration, candidate
experience, onboarding, and measures of team capability. Avoid treating one technology keyword as competence.
