# Scenario 001: Senior-Engineer Architecture Disagreement

Two respected senior engineers disagree. One wants to extract six services immediately; the other wants to keep the
modular monolith for another year. Design work has stalled, and the disagreement is becoming personal.

Explain:

1. what you do in the next 48 hours;
2. which evidence and decision criteria you require;
3. who owns the decision and how dissent is handled;
4. how you preserve trust and delivery;
5. how you verify the decision later.

Do not solve the architecture without first explaining the decision process.
