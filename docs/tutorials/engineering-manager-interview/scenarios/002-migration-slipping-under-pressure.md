# Scenario 002: Migration Slipping Under Pressure

A monolith-to-service migration was announced for the quarter. Shadow comparison still shows 2% unexplained decision
differences, and the backfill stresses the production database. An executive asks you to keep the date because a major
contract depends on the new scale.

Explain the evidence you gather, options you present, recommendation, immediate technical controls, revised success
gates, and communication cadence. Address both business urgency and the danger of transferring authority too early.
