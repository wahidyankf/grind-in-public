# Scenario 008: Noisy Enterprise Tenant

One large tenant's bulk replay saturates shared consumers and raises decision latency for everyone. The tenant is
strategic and was informally promised unlimited processing. Product wants no rejection; on-call wants to stop the
replay.

Explain immediate mitigation, fairness policy, technical isolation, contract/product alignment, capacity evidence,
communication, and longer-term architecture. Distinguish critical online work from resumable bulk work.
