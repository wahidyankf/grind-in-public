# Scenario 004: Repeated On-Call Incidents

The team has had six night-time incidents in eight weeks. Most involve retry storms and database pool exhaustion after
dependency slowdown. Feature work continues to receive priority, and two engineers carry most on-call knowledge.

Explain immediate safety actions, reliability prioritization, technical and process changes, on-call capability
building, stakeholder communication, and the measurements that show improvement.
