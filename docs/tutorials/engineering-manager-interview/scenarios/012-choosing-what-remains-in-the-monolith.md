Leadership expects the migration to end with every module as a service. Your evidence shows case workflow and profile
management share transactions, ownership, and scaling characteristics and would become chatty if split.

Explain the target architecture, evidence, decision criteria, risks of keeping versus extracting, internal modularity
controls, future triggers, and how you communicate that a retained modular monolith can be a deliberate completed state.
