# Scenario 006: Underperformance with Unclear Expectations

An engineer misses commitments and needs extensive rework. Their prior manager gave only positive feedback, and the
current role expectations are poorly documented. The team is frustrated and asks you to act quickly.

Explain how you establish evidence, clarify expectations, hear context, protect confidentiality, support improvement,
set checkpoints, and handle team impact. Address what responsibility management bears for the ambiguity.
