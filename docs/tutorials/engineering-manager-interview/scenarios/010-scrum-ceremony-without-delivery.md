# Scenario 010: Scrum Ceremony without Delivery

The team holds every Scrum event and reports stable velocity, yet work takes weeks to finish. Reviews queue behind two
senior engineers, urgent support interrupts every sprint, and stories roll over without learning.

Explain what data you inspect, which flow changes you trial, how roles and goals change, how support work becomes
visible, and how you avoid treating velocity as the outcome.
