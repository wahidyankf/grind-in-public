# Scenario 005: Strong Engineer with Collaboration Problems

A high-output senior engineer dismisses questions in reviews, rewrites others' work, and makes architecture decisions in
private. Several teammates now avoid proposing ideas. The engineer believes urgency and quality justify the behaviour.

Give the feedback conversation, expectations, support, follow-up evidence, consequences if behaviour persists, and how
you repair team participation without publicly shaming the engineer.
