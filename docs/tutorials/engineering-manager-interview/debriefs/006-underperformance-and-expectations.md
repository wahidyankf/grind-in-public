# Debrief 006: Underperformance with Unclear Expectations

Management owns the prior ambiguity. Do not retroactively punish the engineer for undocumented standards. Gather recent
work evidence and define the role expectation with examples: planning reliability, review quality, autonomy, or
technical scope. Discuss the gap privately, hear workload, health, access, and skill context, and align with
organizational policy.

Create a bounded plan with observable work outcomes, support, weekly checkpoints, and written summaries. Support may
include smaller scoped ownership, pairing, technical training, clearer priorities, or reduced concurrent work. The plan
must still state what sustained improvement looks like and what happens if it does not occur.

Protect the team by redistributing critical work transparently without disclosing private details, and recognize the
temporary load. Fix role documentation and feedback cadence for everyone.

Weak answers immediately label the person, compare them publicly, wait months to avoid discomfort, or make a vague
"communicate better" plan that cannot be evaluated.
