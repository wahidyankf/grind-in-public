# Debrief 002: Migration Slipping Under Pressure

The 2% mismatch is not "almost done" until classified by business consequence, tenant, rule, and version. Pause
expansion, protect the database with snapshot/object backfill or replica capacity, and assign owners to mismatch
categories.

Present options with impact:

- keep legacy authority, deliver capacity through quotas/scale, and continue shadow;
- canary only cohorts whose equivalence and capacity gates pass;
- reduce contract scope to a safe path;
- transfer early only with explicit executive risk acceptance when rollback and data authority remain safe.

Recommend no general authority transfer until unexplained action mismatches reach the agreed threshold, backfill
converges, new path passes peak load/SLO, and rollback is exercised. Publish a reforecast based on these states, not
optimistic task percentages.

Communicate daily during critical convergence and at each gate. Strong answers respect the contract's business urgency
by proposing a smaller safe outcome. Weak answers say only "quality takes time" or accept the date without explaining
the irreversible risk.
