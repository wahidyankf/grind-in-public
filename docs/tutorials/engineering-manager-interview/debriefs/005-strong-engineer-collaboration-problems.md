# Debrief 005: Strong Engineer with Collaboration Problems

Meet promptly and privately. Cite specific observed instances, impact on participation/ownership, and the senior-level
expectation: ask before taking over, critique the work not person, publish decisions, and grow others. Listen for
context without debating the reported impact.

Agree observable changes: invite a peer to lead the next review, write alternatives before deciding, wait for questions,
and mentor without rewriting. Offer coaching and regular feedback; gather evidence from direct observation and multiple
sources, not anonymous rumor as the sole basis.

Restore team mechanisms: facilitated reviews, explicit decision owners, round-robin input, and recognition of others'
work. Do not reveal the private conversation. If behaviour persists, handle it as performance against role expectations
with documented checkpoints and consequences.

Weak answers excuse harm because output is high, publicly shame the engineer, or move all technical authority away
without giving a fair chance to change.
