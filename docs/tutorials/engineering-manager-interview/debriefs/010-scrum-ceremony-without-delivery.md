# Debrief 010: Scrum Ceremony without Delivery

Inspect cycle time distribution, work age, blocked time, review queue age, rollover, support arrival, rework, and
outcome completion. Stable velocity can coexist with slicing games and unfinished value.

Trial lower WIP, smaller end-to-end slices, swarming to finish, explicit review WIP/SLA, and reviewer development so two
seniors are not the gate. Create a support service class with rotation and capacity policy; urgent work has visible cost
and entry criteria. Protect a sprint/product goal or choose continuous flow if interruption is the dominant reality.

Managers remove systemic blockers such as environment or approval delays. Retrospectives choose one owned experiment and
evaluate it with cycle/work age and delivered outcomes.

Do not add more ceremonies or demand higher velocity. Weak answers blame estimation or force overtime without changing
the queueing system.
