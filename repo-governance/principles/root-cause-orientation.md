# Root Cause Orientation

When an error, mistake, or failure is found, investigate its root cause and fix it at the responsible layer. Do not
ignore, suppress, or leave the cause in place merely to make the immediate symptom disappear. If a durable fix requires
owner direction or exceeds the task scope, report the evidence, impact, and recommended next step.
