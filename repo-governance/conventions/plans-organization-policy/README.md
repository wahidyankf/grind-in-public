# Plans Organization Policy Details

These modules hold the detail behind the [plans organization policy](../plans-organization-policy.md). Modules `001`
through `010` are this repository's copy of the portable plan system; `011` through `017` are its own additions. The
numbers are the reading order, and later modules assume earlier ones — see the
[document naming policy](../document-naming-policy.md) for why they are numbered at all.

## Contents

1. [Lifecycle and Folders](001-lifecycle-and-folders.md) — the four `plans/` roots and the slug each carries.
2. [Lifecycle Moves](002-lifecycle-moves.md) — promoting, starting, returning, completing, and reopening a plan.
3. [Required Documents](003-required-documents.md) — the six documents and what each owns.
4. [Technical Shape and Companions](004-technical-shape-and-companions.md) — one shape, and how companions are named.
5. [Delivery Contract](005-delivery-contract.md) — one action per checkbox, execution-grade clarity, executor tags.
6. [Workflows and Skills](006-workflows-and-skills.md) — the seven lifecycle capabilities and who owns each.
7. [Structural Validation](007-structural-validation.md) — what a validator checks, and what it must not.
8. [Evidence and Quality](008-evidence-and-quality.md) — terminal verdicts, bounded repair, sanitized evidence.
9. [Knowledge Capture and Archival](009-knowledge-capture-and-archival.md) — draining `learnings.md`, then filing.
10. [Portability](010-portability.md) — the self-containment rule and this repository's recorded deviations.
11. [Phases and Gates](011-phases-and-gates.md) — phase gates and Pause Safety notes.
12. [Execution Record](012-execution-record.md) — the dated record of phases, gates, failures, and retries.
13. [Two-Pager Template](013-two-pager-template.md) — the eight sections of an idea brief.
14. [Specification Changes](014-specification-changes.md) — prospective C4, Gherkin, test, and File Impact deltas.
15. [Plan Migrations](015-plan-migrations.md) — preservation and recovery for data-bearing transitions.
16. [Plan UI Design](016-plan-ui-design.md) — responsive, accessible alternatives and selected-device proof.
17. [Plan Document Safety](017-plan-document-safety.md) — ASCII-only diagrams and secret-free plan records.
